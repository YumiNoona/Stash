# Botanical card artwork

These four card designs are original, deterministic SVG artwork authored for Stash.
They use editable vector paths and restrained gradients, with no generated mascot
imagery, text, chips, numbers, or brand marks baked into the backgrounds.

- `card_solar_v4.svg`: Coral Bloom — mint ground with overlapping coral/pink tulip petals. Light lettering flag: false.
- `card_citrus_v4.svg`: Sea Petals — pale blue ground with teal, pink, and blue leaves. Light lettering flag: false.
- `card_ember_v4.svg`: Night Fern — deep forest ground with mint and coral leaves. Light lettering flag: true.
- `card_mosaic_v4.svg`: Petal Pop — blush ground with an oversized blue flower and small peach counterpoint. Light lettering flag: false.

1280 × 807 master art. WEBP exports are rendered from these SVGs with Sharp/librsvg
at double resolution and downsampled for smooth edges. Card details and EMV chips
are drawn separately by the app.
