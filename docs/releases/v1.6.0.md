# Stash v1.6.0

- Float the translucent dock above scrolling content without a reserved rectangular footer.
- Add resizable wallet and document-folder home-screen widgets, with a fan-out opening animation inside Stash after unlocking. Add widgets from Settings. Launcher previews use generic art and counts, never private values.
- Keep imported or scanned image pages together in one document, with swipe navigation, page counts, zoom, reorder, rotation and page removal. Share or download multi-image documents as one PDF; bulk downloads use ZIP.
- Add School, Personal and custom folders; create, rename, or remove folders while keeping their documents. Select multiple documents to move, download or delete. Undo list deletions. Recover folder labels from restored vault records.
- Give duplicates numbered names such as `Homework (1).pdf`, preserving extensions and resolving conflicts during import, rename and moves.
- Give recent items an actions menu with Open, Information, Move, Download and Delete, where appropriate to the item.
- Make File information metadata-only and remove individual button backgrounds from the floating document toolbar.
- Show art in the Documents and Cards add banners and remove their arrow badges.
- Enter card details first, then choose art and motion. Back preserves fields; Skip saves a basic card. Nothing is saved until the final action.
- Replace Coral Bloom, Sea Petals, Night Fern, Petal Pop and Opal with four original raster artworks plus a basic finish. Keep the other approved designs. Rework motion into softer seamless glints, foil, silk, glow and ambient effects without moving card text.

Validation: 34 JVM tests; Android lint has no errors; Android 15 emulator checks cover grouped three-image import, page swiping/reordering, metadata-only information, multi-page PDF sharing, bulk moves and card appearance flow. Artwork prompts and resource paths are recorded in `docs/card-art-v6-sources.json` (built-in imagegen).

The APK uses the same local debug signing identity as previous Stash releases so it can update those installs. Widget opening animation runs inside Stash, after the launcher hands off and the app lock is satisfied; it respects Reduce motion.
