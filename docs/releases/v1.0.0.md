# Stash 1.0.0 — Keep life together

A calmer home for your cards, documents, IDs, passwords and notes. Beautifully organized, encrypted on your Android phone, and available without an account or internet connection.

## Your everyday essentials, together

- **A personal card wallet:** landscape previews, separate partner and bank details, distinctive artwork, subtle motion and custom image, GIF or silent video backgrounds.
- **Documents without the pile:** scan or import images, PDFs and text; keep pages together; organize folders and tags; select, move, download and share.
- **Identity details close at hand:** Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID and driving licence front/back views, editable fields, your own portrait and original scans.
- **Small tools that help:** on-device text recognition, notes, a local password generator, search, home-screen widgets and explicit exports.
- **A comfortable interface:** floating glass controls, Space Grotesk, aurora backgrounds and soft light/dark themes.

## A production build

This APK is signed with the production key, non-debuggable, and optimized with R8 and resource shrinking. Unused artwork, fonts and historical screenshots have been removed. Bundled PNG artwork is now lossless WebP with exact pixel checks. Card motion updates drawing layers without recomposing the full card contents every frame.

The repository includes a refreshed README, six real production-app screenshots with fictional data, and a complete responsive website in `web/`.

## Install

Download **Stash-v1.0.0.apk** below. Requires **Android 8.0 or newer**. The adjacent `.sha256` file lets you verify the download. No accounts, analytics, cloud sync or internet permission. New vaults start empty.

**Earlier test-build users:** this production key differs from the old debug signing key. Android cannot update that installation directly. Export and verify readable copies and original files before changing installations. Uninstalling deletes the local vault and its device-bound key; an encrypted backup cannot recover the deleted key. See the repository's build guide before proceeding.

Encrypted backups currently restore only where the original key is still available. OCR needs review; identity views are personal records, not issued or verified digital IDs. Their QR links only to venusapp.in.
