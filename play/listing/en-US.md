# Stash

**Short description**

Your private space for cards, documents, passwords and notes. Works offline.

**Full description**

Keep the things you need close, in one private space on your Android phone.

Stash helps you organize cards, identity details, documents, passwords and notes without creating an account or relying on cloud sync. Add a document with your camera, photos or files; review each page, correct its edges, and read text on your device. Find what you saved with search, folders and custom tags.

Make your cards easier to recognize with colorful artwork, then choose when to reveal sensitive details. Generate strong passwords locally. Add a wallet or document folder widget to your home screen for quick access.

Your vault records and attachments are encrypted in app-private storage. Share or export only when you choose. A portable encrypted backup lets you move your vault with a passphrase you control. No ads, analytics, account or internet permission.

Features:
• Camera scans, photo and file import, including images and PDFs
• Local text recognition and document review
• Card, identity, password and note storage
• Search, folders, tags and home-screen widgets
• Light and dark themes, custom backgrounds and card artwork
• Encrypted local storage and passphrase-protected portable backup

Stash is a personal organizer. It does not issue identity documents, connect to banks or guarantee NFC compatibility with every card.
