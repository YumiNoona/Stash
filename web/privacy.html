Privacy policy.

Stash is an offline Android vault. It has no account, ads, analytics, server, cloud sync or internet permission.

What you choose to store

You may add cards, identity details, documents, photos, passwords, notes, profile details and custom artwork. Camera, selected files and NFC are used only when you choose those features. Text recognition runs on your device. This information is not sent to Veil.

Storage and protection

Vault records and attachments are encrypted with AES-GCM in app-private storage. A device-bound key is held by Android Keystore. Profile name, photo and display settings are kept separately in app-private storage. A portable backup is encrypted with a key derived from your chosen passphrase. Veil cannot access or recover your vault or a forgotten backup passphrase.

Sharing and exports

Stash sends data to another app only when you choose Share, save a file, or export. The receiving app or location then controls that copy. Summary exports omit names, free-form details and file names. Full readable exports may contain private data and are not encrypted. Card verification codes are never included in readable exports.

Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall Stash. Uninstalling deletes the local key and vault. User-created exports and shared copies outside Stash must be removed separately. Temporary share files in Stash's cache are removed after about ten minutes or on a later app launch. Android automatic cloud and device-transfer backup are disabled.
